## Photo precision & omissions
- `assets/original_photos/` retains all supplied 18 originals, including the two price lists. In app: Profile → Original uniform photos.
- `assets/products/` contains nine AI-assisted polished garment renderings, reused where photographed matches permit; four visible **illustrative PNGs** were added for unverified standalone items, instead of falsely claiming a real garment reference.
- Neckwear illustrations do *not* establish that the photo ties are the same as their separately priced accessories.
- Some source shots contain multiple garments (e.g. vest over blouse) rather than single standalone pieces.
- Image-to-school-level matches for H.S. Blouse, NEU Skirt, College Skirt and College Pants require confirmation.
- Handwritten 5XL/6XL and obscured accessories/fabric/P.E. prices deliberately omitted.

For a production deployment, get supplier approval and photograph every item separately, front and back, with actual label and measurement chart.
